# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Lhie Store serves two primary groups:

- Customers who want ready-to-use AI and digital premium accounts, discover products through the web storefront, then complete support-heavy purchase flows through Telegram.
- Operators, admins, support, and finance users who manage catalog items, stock, orders, payment confirmation, receipts, broadcast channels, and operational follow-up from the admin dashboard.

Customers are usually trying to buy quickly, understand what they get, trust that the account is ready, and know where to get help. Admin users are trying to keep stock and orders accurate while responding fast to Telegram-driven demand.

## Product Purpose

Lhie Store is a hybrid storefront and Telegram commerce experience for AI and digital premium accounts. The web surface presents products, guidance, trust signals, and checkout entry points; Telegram remains a core purchase, support, and fulfillment channel.

Success means customers can confidently choose the right account, understand the next payment or Telegram step, and complete the purchase without confusion, while admins can keep the commerce operation reliable and easy to manage.

## Brand Personality

Playful neo-brutal: bold, energetic, direct, and transaction-focused. The brand should feel young and memorable without becoming unserious, noisy, or hard to use.

The voice should be friendly and practical: clear product promises, confident support cues, simple checkout guidance, and minimal jargon.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS templates: dark purple-blue gradients, endless identical cards, vague AI-startup copy, and decorative dashboards that do not help customers buy.
- Overly premium minimalism: sterile, quiet, luxury-style restraint that erases the playful Lhie Store energy.
- Chaotic neo-brutalism: excessive color, motion, emoji, or visual noise that competes with product selection, payment instructions, or admin workflows.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next purchase step unmistakable: every surface should clarify whether the user should browse, choose, pay, contact Telegram, or wait for fulfillment.
2. Keep the playful edge useful: bold neo-brutal styling should create recognition and confidence, not distraction.
3. Design for trust before decoration: stock status, payment state, support routes, product details, and receipt cues must be more prominent than ornamental effects.
4. Separate customer energy from operator clarity: storefront surfaces can be more expressive, while admin surfaces must stay dense, legible, and fast to scan.
5. Preserve Telegram as a first-class channel: copy, CTAs, empty states, and error states should make Telegram support and fulfillment feel intentional, not like a workaround.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for customer and admin surfaces. Maintain strong text contrast, visible keyboard focus, readable mobile layouts, clear form labels and error states, and reduced-motion alternatives for decorative or reveal animations.
