# LhieStore Bot

This context describes the store domain language used by the web storefront and Telegram bot, especially ordering, catalog access, and public sales-proof communication.

## Language

**Sales Proof Post**:
A public Telegram channel post that announces a successfully paid customer order using masked customer information.
_Avoid_: Testimonial, review

**Store Bot**:
The Telegram bot customers use to browse products and place orders.
_Avoid_: Channel

**Admin Broadcast**:
An intentionally confirmed message delivered by an authorized admin through the Store Bot to eligible Telegram customers. Its admin-authored text or one photo with an optional caption remains verbatim; localized Store Bot title, footer, and optional product-action button wrappers may surround that content per recipient locale.
_Avoid_: Info from Admin, automatic product notification

**Web Storefront**:
The public website where customers browse the catalog, check out with QRIS, and access paid digital-account data without a Telegram dependency.
_Avoid_: Telegram checkout
**QRIS Cross-Border**:
The QRIS interoperability connection between Indonesia and participating partner countries that lets supported payment apps and merchants transact across linked QR systems. Availability depends on the payment provider and participating merchant; it is not universal country coverage.
_Avoid_: QRIS available in every country, QRIS for Indonesian apps only

**Native QRIS**:
A QRIS payment generated by an automatic provider, with provider final payable total/deadline. New checkout currently uses KlikQRIS; historical Pakasir Native QRIS Payments remain immutable compatibility records. It excludes QR Manual, new Pakasir hosted checkout, and Binance Pay.
_Avoid_: QRIS image, automatic QRIS without a provider total

**Late Payment**:
A provider-confirmed payment received after the local Order has already become terminal, such as Expired or Cancelled. It is financially valid but does not authorize automatic fulfillment; it requires manual review before fulfillment or refund.
_Avoid_: ordinary Paid Order, late checkout

**Retired Credential**:
A digital-account credential that has been delivered or otherwise exposed and must never be returned to the Available Product pool, including after a refund.
_Avoid_: reusable refunded credential, restocked account

**Product Stock Item**:
One sellable credential unit for a Product that is either available, reserved to an Order, permanently allocated, or retired. A Product Stock Item is distinct from the Product's numeric stock projection.
_Avoid_: stock row, quantity line

**Stock Intake**:
An Owner/Admin-confirmed operation that adds one or more Product Stock Items and their matching inventory adjustment as one indivisible change.
_Avoid_: line count, direct stock edit

**Structured Account Credential**:
A Product Stock Item with a required email and password, at most one optional access method, and an optional item-specific tutorial. It is an alternative to a legacy free-form credential, not a replacement for it.
_Avoid_: free-form stock line, customer account

**OTP Mail Link**:
An encrypted HTTPS destination delivered with one Structured Account Credential so its customer can open the associated OTP mailbox. It is an access action, not a copyable authentication secret or Product usage guide.
_Avoid_: Auth Token, tutorial link

**Auth Token**:
An encrypted copyable Outlook/Hotmail authentication secret delivered as text with one Structured Account Credential. It is an alternative to an OTP Mail Link.
_Avoid_: OTP Mail Link, usage guide

**Provider Cancellation Attempt**:
An idempotent post-commit request to cancel an unpaid provider transaction after the local Order has been released. It does not reverse a provider-confirmed payment; such a payment becomes a Late Payment.
_Avoid_: local cancellation, guaranteed provider refund

**Payment Verification Pending**:
A state where a requested provider status cannot yet be established authoritatively. The local Order remains unchanged and is retried through reconciliation rather than being released or fulfilled.
_Avoid_: assumed unpaid, verification failure treated as cancellation

**Manual Fulfillment Review**:
The fulfillment state for a financially valid payment whose Order cannot be fulfilled automatically, requiring an owner decision such as fulfill, refund, or reject.
_Avoid_: failed payment, ordinary fulfillment

**Customer**:
The canonical buyer account that owns Carts, Orders, Store Balance and Customer USDT Balance. Existing Store Bot identity is its numeric `telegram_id`; Web Storefront accounts may have nullable email and Telegram ID. Authentication linking never merges Customers or moves their financial or Order ownership.
_Avoid_: Admin User, OAuth account, automatically merged customer

**Customer Identity**:
A verified external provider subject bound to one Customer. The first release allows Google and Telegram, one identity per provider per Customer; provider email, username and support contact are not linking authority. A Telegram identity must agree with that same Customer's canonical Store Bot identity.
_Avoid_: Provider access token, support contact, second customer account

**Customer Authentication Challenge**:
A purpose-bound, hashed, expiring account proof with durable consumption/attempt state. Its verification, OTP, reset or Telegram purpose cannot grant another operation. An anonymous Telegram challenge binds its originating browser before private-chat proof identifies the existing Customer.
_Avoid_: Recovery Key, Product Auth Token, Order ownership proof

**Recovery Key**:
A one-time-displayed customer secret paired with an invoice number to reopen paid digital-account data in the Web Storefront. Only its hash is stored with the order.
_Avoid_: Password reset token, resendable key

**Optional Support Contact**:
An order-level Telegram or WhatsApp contact supplied during Web Storefront checkout only so support can follow up on operational help. It is not customer-account identity and is not evidence of order ownership.
_Avoid_: Owner contact, login contact

**Credential Delivery Email**:
The paid-account copy sent to the optional checkout email address of a Web Storefront Order once payment is confirmed and the credentials are releasable. It is a convenience channel, never an ownership claim: the address is snapshotted when the delivery is recorded, cannot be re-pointed afterwards, and the same credentials stay visible on the Order invoice through the Recovery Key. Retrying or resending always targets that snapshot.
_Avoid_: Recovery Key delivery, login email, requirement for receiving credentials

**Store Channel**:
The optional Telegram channel promoted from the Store Bot home, where sales proof, stock updates, promotions, and owner announcements are published. Store Bot access never depends on channel membership.
_Avoid_: Community, group, access gate

**Paid Order Proof Event**:
The moment a public sales proof may be created: after payment is confirmed paid and the inventory sale has been recorded.
_Avoid_: Completed order event

**Available Product**:
A product that can currently be bought because it has available stock.
_Avoid_: In-stock item

**Unavailable Product**:
A product that remains visible in the catalog but cannot currently be bought because it has no available stock.
_Avoid_: Hidden product, deleted product

**Product Category**:
An admin-managed catalog grouping used to organize products for customer browsing. Its name, description, customer visibility, and display order can be changed. It can be soft-deleted without deleting its products; its slug is system-managed.
_Avoid_: Product type, product collection

**Product Family**:
A stable customer-facing discovery grouping for compatible Products. It owns identity, ordering, description, and presentation only; price, stock, credentials, checkout, and fulfillment remain on each Product.
_Avoid_: sellable Variant, shared inventory

**Product option**:
An existing sellable Product/SKU shown as a member of a Product Family. Its Product ID remains the identity submitted to cart, checkout, order, inventory, waitlist, and delivery flows.
_Avoid_: family-level stock, new Variant entity

**Plan**:
The customer-facing name for one Product option inside a Product Family, used by the Store Bot catalog and the Web Storefront. It is presentation vocabulary only: the sellable identity remains the Product option, and a Product Family with a single Plan opens that Product Detail directly instead of listing its Plans. Product Detail opened from a family's Plan list returns to that Plan list instead of the catalog list.
_Avoid_: Variant, SKU, subscription tier

**Family catalog icon**:
The presentation values a Product Family owns for its whole product line: an optional Unicode glyph plus an optional captured Telegram custom emoji identifier. The Store Bot icon screen captures it once per family, and the Admin Dashboard can edit the glyph. The family heading always uses this pair. Every member Product that stores no icon of its own inherits it for Plan rows/buttons, public stock rows, variant detail, notifications, and Order-item snapshots; a member override replaces the pair on those member surfaces without changing the heading. It stays on the family instead of being copied onto each member, so changing it updates every inheriting member without overwriting a member's own icon.
_Avoid_: Derived family icon, per-variant icon copy

**Product catalog icon**:
The presentation values a Product owns for its catalog entry: an optional Unicode glyph retained as the Web Storefront and Telegram fallback, plus an optional Telegram custom emoji identifier. Telegram message text renders a captured identifier with `<tg-emoji emoji-id="...">glyph</tg-emoji>` and some inline keyboard buttons carry it in `icon_custom_emoji_id` when Telegram allows custom emoji; the Web Storefront and rejected/unsupported Telegram sends use the Unicode glyph. Capturing a premium emoji in the Store Bot stores its identifier and Telegram's associated Unicode `emoji` as that fallback; sending a plain emoji stores the glyph directly. The Admin Dashboard can edit the glyph afterward. Telegram permits custom emoji entities in direct private/group/supergroup bot messages when the bot owner has Telegram Premium; channel posts instead require the bot's Fragment-purchased additional username entitlement, and until the bot has it Telegram accepts the post while silently dropping the emoji entities and button icons, so a Store Channel post keeps its Unicode glyph with no error to observe. Inside a Product Family this pair is the Product's own override: Telegram Plan rows, variant detail and quantity prompts, notifications, cart, broadcast, stock list, price-change snapshots, and immutable Order-item presentation snapshots all render the effective icon — the Product's own pair when it stores either value, otherwise the icon its Product Family owns (see **Family catalog icon**). Plan-selection buttons use a small Unicode right-pointing marker (`▸`) instead of that premium icon. It is presentation only: it never changes price, stock, checkout, or fulfillment. It is not a secret and must not be configured through ENV.
_Avoid_: Brand icon, automatic brand detection, sticker pack

**Telegram UI icon**:
A named Store Bot navigation slot (home, back, product catalog, balance, orders, support, language, channel) that may carry a captured Telegram custom emoji on its button. The plain Unicode glyph stays in the locale copy and is dropped only while a captured identifier renders. It is presentation only: the callback behaviour of the button never changes.
_Avoid_: menu sticker, navigation setting

**Telegram message-copy icon**:
A named `message.*` slot in `telegram_ui_icons` that maps a legacy static-copy glyph to an editable Unicode fallback and optional captured custom emoji ID. `messageicon:` lets an admin capture premium or plain emoji. Translation templates resolve these slots before replacement values are inserted; code templates render static prefixes only, leaving customer, Product, payment, and credential values untouched. Product and family icons remain catalog-owned.
_Avoid_: Product icon, user-authored text

**Uncategorized Product**:
An active product that is not assigned to a Product Category. Deleting a Product Category moves its products into this state without changing their active status or sale availability.
_Avoid_: Deleted product, inactive product

**Binance Payment Verification**:
The process of checking a Binance Pay transfer against a pending payment before the order is completed, using the expected amount, currency, configured receiver, and, when required, the Binance Order ID. It can confirm the payment, leave it awaiting more evidence, or send it for manual review.
_Avoid_: Binance payment proof (that means screenshot evidence)

**Binance Payment Rail**:
A customer-selected payment path under Binance checkout: Binance Pay ID or USDT BEP20 on BNB Smart Chain. The selected rail determines the payment instructions and the authoritative verification source for that Payment.
_Avoid_: Binance method when the specific rail is unknown, Binance Pay for a BSC transfer

**BSC Payment Verification**:
The process of checking a USDT BEP20 transfer on BNB Smart Chain against the immutable receiver, token contract, expected amount, payment window, and confirmation policy of a pending Payment. A transaction hash is evidence to look up, not proof accepted without on-chain validation.
_Avoid_: BSC screenshot verification, explorer-page verification, Binance Pay verification

**BSC Receiving Address**:
An Owner-managed public BNB Smart Chain address that can be assigned to a new USDT BEP20 Payment. Its address, network, and token contract are snapshotted on assignment so later address rotation does not change an existing Payment.
_Avoid_: Binance Pay ID, wallet credential, private key

**Payment Provider Switch**:
The owner-controlled store setting selects Manual, KlikQRIS, or Sakurupiah for new checkouts without changing Payments already created with another provider. A provider must pass its readiness gate before selection; Sakurupiah production requires both production credentials. Pakasir is not selectable for new checkout; historical Pakasir Payments remain on their original provider.
_Avoid_: Customer provider choice, automatic provider fallback

**Checkout Maintenance**:
The Owner/Admin-controlled store state that temporarily prevents new customer Orders while catalog access, cart management, and existing Order lifecycles remain available.
_Avoid_: Store Maintenance, Store Closure

**Telegram Outbound Effect**:
A durable business delivery unit addressed to a Telegram customer, administrator, recipient, or Store Channel. It represents a customer-visible or operationally important message/media delivery, not every ephemeral Telegram API call.
_Avoid_: Telegram API request, background message

**Ambiguous Telegram Delivery**:
An outbound effect whose Telegram outcome cannot be proven because the request or response ended in a timeout, connection failure, or worker interruption. It is not treated as a definite send failure.
_Avoid_: ordinary send failure, retryable Telegram error

**Manual Delivery Review**:
The operator-controlled state for an Ambiguous Telegram Delivery that must be checked in Telegram or deliberately resent as a new effect before it is considered delivered.
_Avoid_: Manual Fulfillment Review, automatic retry

**Store Balance**:
A Customer-owned IDR balance backed by an immutable ledger and an atomically maintained projection that can be reconstructed from its entries. It is spent as a full-payment checkout rail and funded through provider-backed top-ups.
_Avoid_: Wallet, deposit balance, credits, loyalty points

**Store Balance Funding Policy**:
The versioned, Admin-managed minimum and ordered preset amounts used to present and validate provider-backed top-ups. It is configuration only: changing it never creates a Store Balance Entry or changes a Customer projection.
_Avoid_: balance credit, ledger entry, provider quote

**Store Balance Funding Intent**:
A Customer-owned, expiring confirmation record that binds one normalized credit amount to the current funding-policy version, private chat, canonical navigation root, and opaque callback nonce. It is a pre-provider confirmation only: it is not a Payment, invoice, provider transaction, Store Balance Entry, or balance credit.
_Avoid_: invoice, deposit balance, provider quote

**Store Balance Top-up**:
A Customer-owned, one-time funding record created from a confirmed Store Balance Funding Intent on the store's currently selected payment provider rail (`manual`, `klikqris`, or `sakurupiah`). It keeps the requested credit amount separate from the provider-authoritative fee, final payable total, QR data, expiry, and settlement evidence; retries and recovery reuse the same merchant reference and idempotency identity. It is not an Order, Payment, invoice, or balance credit until authenticated provider success is settled. Because the provider is captured when the top-up is confirmed, the Payment Provider Switch applies to new top-ups only.
_Avoid_: invoice, Payment, merchandise Order, provider-neutral QRIS target

**Manual Store Balance Top-up**:
A Store Balance Top-up created on the `manual` rail that starts awaiting operator confirmation: the customer receives the store's static QR instructions with the credit amount and deadline, and an Owner/Admin/Finance operator confirms or rejects it from the admin Telegram notification. Rejection or deadline expiry ends it without a credit; only a confirmed operator decision settles it.
_Avoid_: automatic settlement, provider-confirmed top-up, ordinary pending Payment

**Store Balance Top-up Provider Snapshot**:
The provider together with the payment setting identity and version recorded on a Store Balance Top-up at funding confirmation. Every later lifecycle, presentation, recovery, settlement, and expiry decision reads this snapshot, so changing the Payment Provider Switch never rewrites, re-targets, or invalidates an existing top-up.
_Avoid_: live provider selection, retroactive provider change, automatic provider fallback

**Store Balance Late Settlement**:
The settlement of one Store Balance Top-Up from trusted provider success observed after the top-up deadline had already passed, without waiting for an Owner compensation. It keeps the single credit identity, never reopens a compensated top-up, and is recorded apart from an in-window settlement.
_Avoid_: Late Payment, compensation release, second credit

**Store Balance Top-Up Proof Post**:
A public Telegram channel post that announces a successfully settled Store Balance Top-Up using the same masked-buyer invoice photo and caption treatment as an order sales proof. It never publishes raw identifiers and never changes the published successful-order number.
_Avoid_: Top-up receipt, sales report, revenue post

**Store Balance Entry**:
One immutable ledger row recording a single economic effect on a Store Balance — a top-up credit, a purchase debit, or a refund credit — with its immutable source identity and idempotency identity. Reapplying the same source or key returns the existing entry.
_Avoid_: Transaction, balance mutation, adjustment row

The following balance-extension terms originate in the **proposed** contracts under [ADR-0019](docs/adr/0019-idr-usdt-economic-sources-and-receipt-claims.md), issue #537, and the A1 capability report. They describe downstream contracts, not currently available features or approved funding providers. Store Balance continues to mean the existing IDR ledger; the external money receipt registry and claim seam are implemented additively (R1 #541) and R2 #542 backfills legacy identities, routes every external payment writer through the registry, and records ambiguous evidence as reservations without activating funding. I1 #543 implements the IDR adjustment service under the accepted H0-v1 single-Owner overlay; I2 #544 adds a gated IDR Dashboard flow; I3 #545 adds the linked full reversal under the same password boundary; U1 #546 adds the separate Customer USDT account, immutable ledger, accounting events and exact micro-USDT contract while funding stays disabled. R2 implementation decisions are recorded in [ADR-0022](docs/adr/0022-external-money-receipt-backfill-and-reservations.md). U4 #549 implements the global receiver-ownership registry — permanent channel-exclusive address claims plus Customer-exclusive deposit receivers — while custody provisioning stays fail-closed and funding remains disabled.

**Customer USDT Balance**:
A Customer-owned shopping balance held in a separate USDT account and immutable ledger, denominated in integer micro-USDT. U7 credits verified final, attributable top-ups atomically with receipt ownership, economic resolution, accounting and durable Telegram receipt work; new funding remains local/testing-only. U10 spends the full frozen USDT quantity-tier price through the internal `usdt_balance` checkout rail, atomically with Order/Payment, inventory, fulfillment work and durable lifecycle notifications. U11 refunds verified warranty/missing-unit entitlements and full-order remainders in original micro-USDT, linked to the original Customer/Payment/debit and capped cumulatively, with immutable accounting and durable exact receipts. Production authorization remains a separate gate. It is a store liability, with no partial tender, implicit conversion to Store Balance or application custody of private keys.
_Avoid_: Store Balance USDT, non-custodial wallet, Supplier Balance

**Customer USDT Balance Entry**:
One immutable ledger row recording a single economic effect on a Customer USDT Balance — a funding credit, purchase debit, refund credit, or linked reversal debit — with its exact positive micro-USDT amount, per-account sequence, typed economic source, request fingerprint and unique idempotency claim. Reapplying the same source, key or fingerprint returns the existing entry; a changed payload conflicts.
_Avoid_: USDT transaction, balance mutation, wallet row

**Customer USDT Balance History**:
The bounded, Customer-scoped presentation read of one Customer's USDT balance activity: immutable ledger entries plus funding targets that have no resolution yet. It is keyset-paginated through a signed, Customer-scoped cursor with a 25-row default and 100-row maximum, and its status text is never money authority — only a linked ledger entry means credited funds.
_Avoid_: account statement, money authority, shared balance cache

**USDT Funding Intent**:
A Customer-owned expiring confirmation binding exact micro-USDT credit, application fee/payable amount, policy/terms versions, exclusive receiver/payment terms, private chat and canonical message identity plus an opaque nonce. U6 persists this preview without provider, invoice, Payment, receipt, ledger or outbound effects; cancellation/expiry ends only the local preview, and a replaced canonical message (not a mere re-render) invalidates it.
_Avoid_: USDT invoice, deposit receipt, available funds

**USDT Top-up**:
A confirmed Customer funding target with an immutable identity, currency, amount, fee, receiver/token/network/finality and payment deadline snapshot, one U3 active-target reservation and durable preparation/settlement work. U6 confirmation replay returns the same target; U5 preparation retains its reference and never switches receivers. U7 accepts authenticated lookup hints into a durable inbox, verifies authoritative chain evidence outside transactions and consumes one receipt under DB lease/version fencing. Exact final late money can settle the original source once; expiry retains unresolved exposure, while settled, compensated and externally returned sources never reopen. Receipt delivery failure cannot change money. Production funding authorization remains a separate gate.
_Avoid_: Order Payment, automatic currency conversion

**USDT Exception Resolution**:
An immutable, single economic resolution of a confirmed USDT Top-up, approved by a fresh Owner per-action password and backed by authoritative finalized transfer evidence. Revised settlement or compensation credits the actual exactly representable deposit less the original fee without rewriting the original invoice; external return requires a distinct verified outgoing receipt and an authenticated Customer destination authorization. Unknown, ambiguous, sub-micro and additional deposits remain unresolved rather than becoming rounded or arbitrary credits.
_Avoid_: invoice overwrite, screenshot approval, manual USDT grant, maker-checker requirement

**USDT Reorg Incident**:
An immutable record that previously credited funding has lost canonical chain evidence, freezing its Customer account while preserving all original receipts and ledger history. Total exposure and conservatively allocated available/spent exposure remain visible as unresolved treasury loss, including when Customer liability is zero.
_Avoid_: automatic chargeback, history deletion, negative Customer balance, settled loss

**USDT Reorg Recovery Case**:
An Owner-authorized, per-action-password-confirmed case for a USDT Reorg Incident. Its `authorized_unresolved` status permits recovery investigation but neither closes the loss nor creates assets, charges the Customer or releases the account freeze.
_Avoid_: automatic recovery, loss settlement, Customer debit authorization

**USDT Funding Policy**:
The versioned DB/Admin configuration for Customer USDT top-up funding: Owner activation flag, bounded confirmation TTL, nonnegative exact application fee, published terms version and Owner pause controls. H0-v1 disables business minimum/maximum/per-Customer/aggregate caps, presets and the beta allowlist, so the policy holds no cap value; representational and overflow guards remain. Changing it never creates a ledger entry, reservation or provider target.
_Avoid_: ENV presentation knob, funding credit, exposure quota

**USDT Funding Reservation**:
The single durable open funding target slot per Customer across rails, created with the policy/terms snapshot and one immutable source identity. Expiry moves it to expired-unresolved but never releases the slot; only a settlement that posts the linked funding ledger entry or a typed release resolution resolves it.
_Avoid_: USDT Top-up, quota bucket, balance hold

**USDT Funding Pause**:
An Owner-controlled operational pause of one USDT scope (funding, spending, one rail, or balance receipt delivery) with a required reason and audit. A funding, rail or delivery pause blocks new targets or their receipt messages only: settlement, refund, reversal and reconciliation of money already observed continue, and a deferred receipt stays durable until it is delivered after resume.
_Avoid_: ledger freeze, account freeze, automatic resume, message loss

**Balance Operations Alert**:
A durable, deduplicated operational alert about Customer USDT Balance evidence — reconciliation drift, receipt collision, treasury coverage, unresolved reorg loss, stuck funding/settlement/delivery work, a frozen account or an open restore gate — announced to the administrator exactly once per episode and fingerprint. It carries counts, sanitized aggregate amounts, run references and scope names only, never customer identity or chain evidence.
_Avoid_: money authority, customer notification, log line

**Balance Restore Gate**:
A post-restore block on new Customer USDT funding and spending after a database restore, kept open until authoritative external evidence for the lost window is replayed and a full reconciliation run recorded after that replay shows zero unexplained drift. It never deletes ledger, receipt or claim history, never refunds automatically and never reopens itself.
_Avoid_: automatic recovery, funding authorization, migration rollback

**BSC Capability Qualification**:
The result of probing the BNB Smart Chain RPC surface required by a future USDT funding rail — chain identity, token contract code/decimals, `finalized` block depth behind head, canonical receipt consistency, and bounded transfer-log access — classified as verified, unsupported, or unknown. Only verified capabilities qualify; mismatch or unknown evidence fails closed. A qualified result is RPC evidence only, never funding, custody, provisioning, or legal authorization.
_Avoid_: provider approval, funding readiness, custody proof

**USDT Deposit Receiver**:
A public BNB Smart Chain address permanently bound to exactly one Customer for USDT top-up funding, with chain, token contract, decimals and custody namespace fixed when the binding is created. Retirement stops new invoice assignment while the binding, the permanent Customer identity and verification evidence stay immutable, and the address keeps being monitored for late money. It is never reassigned to another Customer, never shared with the checkout pool, and never substituted by a shared address with a unique amount. The application stores public addresses and verification evidence only: no private key, seed or signing capability.
_Avoid_: shared deposit address, checkout receiver, wallet credential, private key

**USDT Receiver Address Claim**:
The permanent, channel-exclusive claim of one normalized chain+address pair in the global receiver-ownership registry, owned either by the checkout pool or by one Customer deposit binding. Database uniqueness rejects any cross-table overlap, including addresses whose receiver row was later deleted; a checkout claim can never be converted into a Customer-exclusive binding, and a Customer binding is never recycled.
_Avoid_: address index, soft reservation, customer wallet, shared address pool

**USDT Deposit Receiver Provisioner**:
An approved custody adapter that can answer with one exclusive public address per Customer without the application ever holding key material. It reports readiness and capacity, and when no approved adapter is available or its capacity is exhausted the USDT rail refuses to create an invoice instead of falling back to a shared address.
_Avoid_: signer, wallet generator, private key service

**External Money Receipt**:
A proposed global identity for one externally observed economic transfer, shared by checkout, funding and funded adjustments. Verified aliases refer to the same physical funds; BSC logs also retain a single-target parent transaction claim. Observation is distinct from authorized consumption.
_Avoid_: screenshot, webhook delivery ID, customer-submitted hash proof

**External Money Receipt Backfill**:
A bounded, resumable, additive scan that claims already settled legacy Binance/BSC/QRIS/top-up identities, and records compensated or unnormalizable evidence as reservations, without rewriting any historical amount, provider, or status. Dry runs inspect only and never write receipts, reservations, or checkpoints.
_Avoid_: data migration, status rewrite, money movement

**Legacy Money Receipt Reservation**:
A fail-closed denylist row for legacy evidence that cannot be normalized into a claimed receipt (missing provider identity, conflicts, review holds, or owner-compensated unresolved money). Open blocking reservations stay visible and block the cutover readiness gate instead of being discarded.
_Avoid_: dead letter queue, silent skip, automatic write-off

**Money Receipt Cutover Readiness**:
The reported gate that every backfill source has completed a full scan with zero conflicts, no blocking reservation remains open, and no writer can move external money without a registry claim. USDT funding stays disabled in this release; readiness is not funding authorization.
_Avoid_: production approval, provider readiness, canary

**Balance Adjustment Request**:
An immutable, reasoned and authorized request to add IDR to an already registered Customer selected by exact numeric Telegram ID. I1 #543 implements the service boundary with a five-minute preview and the explicitly approved single-Owner, per-action password confirmation mode from H0-v1; I2 #544 exposes the owner-only Admin Dashboard flow and paginated IDR view; I3 #545 adds the linked full reversal under the same password boundary. Application or linked reversal creates a new ledger effect; it never sets or edits a balance directly. An Owner-attested manual case is distinct from a provider-verified top-up.
_Avoid_: manual balance overwrite, automatic Customer creation, arbitrary USDT grant

**Balance Adjustment Reversal**:
A linked full reversal of one applied Balance Adjustment Request, requested from the original history entry with a fresh Owner password. It debits exactly the original credited amount once, only while available funds cover it; insufficient funds become a review without a partial debit or negative balance. The original entry, linked Top-up/refund resolution and any claimed external receipt stay immutable and resolved.
_Avoid_: balance overwrite, partial reversal, external payment refund

**Economic Source**:
A proposed typed authorization for one ledger effect, binding its immutable business identity, Customer, currency, exact units, evidence and request fingerprint. Matching replay returns the same posting; changed payload conflicts.
_Avoid_: arbitrary source string, transport request ID

**Accounting Event**:
A proposed append-only, currency-specific counterpart record committed with one ledger posting, linking liability, settlement asset, sale, fee or expense legs. Supplier treasury observations and unresolved deposits remain distinct from Customer available funds.
_Avoid_: Telegram receipt, mixed-currency total, projection repair

**Accounting Aggregate**:
A derived, per-consumer total of Accounting Events grouped by balanced leg pair, consumed asynchronously through a unique consumer/event claim. It is reporting evidence that can be rebuilt from immutable events, never an authority for balances and never a single global clearing row.
_Avoid_: source of truth, ledger balance, global clearing account

**Custody Asset Observation**:
A read-only, provider-specific statement of USDT assets held by one approved custody adapter, either proving an exact amount or explicitly reporting unavailable/unsupported/missing input. Unavailable is never a proven zero, and a fixture adapter is never proof of actual backing.
_Avoid_: wallet balance, provider approval, verified backing

**Treasury Movement**:
An audited bookkeeping record of USDT entering or leaving the operator's custody pool outside Customer funding receipts, such as sweeps, external returns or gas paid from custody. Recording it is not a transfer, credit or refund.
_Avoid_: Customer top-up, provider settlement, on-chain transfer

**USDT Reconciliation Run**:
A bounded, read-only comparison of the Customer USDT subledger, its external funding receipts and its accounting aggregates against qualified custody assets and audited treasury movements. Drift freezes only the affected accounts and produces append-only evidence; it never repairs, credits or debits money.
_Avoid_: balance repair, auto-settlement, money movement

**Store Activity**:
The public-safe Telegram home summary of the Telegram Customer count and canonical successful Orders. It never discloses revenue, sold units, GMV, or earnings.
_Avoid_: Sales report, revenue dashboard, store statistics

**Canonical Navigation Message**:
The single bot-owned Telegram message per private Customer chat on which the localized home is shown and where routine navigation (Browse Products, Balance, Orders, Language, Support, Back, Home) is edited in place. Durable transactional artifacts such as QRIS instructions, receipts, delivered credentials, and operational alerts are never used as that message.
_Avoid_: Menu message, home message, reply keyboard menu, pinned message

**Quantity Price Tier**:
A Product-owned pricing rule that replaces the base per-unit catalog amount when one Cart Item reaches its minimum quantity; the applicable rule with the greatest reached minimum wins. It may define paired IDR and USDT amounts, applies only to that Product's quantity, and becomes immutable commercial history only through the Order Item snapshot created at checkout.
_Avoid_: coupon, percentage discount, customer membership tier, Product variant, cart-wide bulk total

**Supplier**:
A private external shop connection from which the store acquires digital products for customer Orders. Its identity, credentials, prices and account balance are operator information, not customer catalog content.
_Avoid_: Payment Provider, customer shop

**Supplier Account Unit**:
One provider-identified digital account acquired for an Order Item, bound to its allocated Product Stock Item, individual supplier warranty deadline and immutable retail entitlement. A replacement retains the same logical entitlement while the original credential remains retired.
_Avoid_: reusable refunded account, supplier stock pool

**Warranty Claim**:
A Customer-owned case about one delivered Supplier Account Unit, with private original proof and an independently verified supplier decision. Claim acceptance is not buyer compensation; an approved claim permits one buyer-selected refund or replacement.
_Avoid_: automatic refund approval, Payment refund

**Order Refund**:
One immutable buyer refund entitlement and settlement record for a verified account claim, definitively missing fulfillment units, or an operator-confirmed remainder. Supplier Balance recovery is separate. A partial Order Refund does not mark the entire Payment Refunded until the complete paid amount is returned.
_Avoid_: supplier credit, proof of bank transfer, direct balance mutation

**Remainder Refund**:
The part of a full external Order refund that no verified account claim or definitively missing fulfillment unit covers, recorded as one confirmed Order Refund from the operator's exact transfer evidence. It never credits Store Balance and never replaces a registered partial refund.
_Avoid_: goodwill credit, adjustment refund, extra refund

**Supplier Balance**:
The store operator's prepaid funds held by a Supplier and available for acquiring products. It is distinct from Customer-owned Store Balance and is not a customer refund destination.
_Avoid_: Store Balance, customer deposit

**Product Supplier Source**:
An operator-approved mapping from one store Product to an equivalent Supplier product, with a purchasing priority. It supplies fulfillment without replacing the Product's customer-facing identity or commercial history.
_Avoid_: duplicated Product, supplier-branded Plan

**Supplier Availability**:
A time-limited estimate of external units that the store can acquire, constrained by supplier stock, Supplier Balance, existing commitments and the store's pricing policy. It is not locally owned stock or a supplier-guaranteed reservation.
_Avoid_: Stock Intake, owned inventory

**Supplier Fulfillment**:
The acquisition and delivery obligation for the externally sourced units of an Order Item after customer payment. Payment confirmation and fulfillment completion are separate facts.
_Avoid_: Payment, supplier checkout

**Supplier Purchase**:
One durable acquisition attempt against a specific Supplier for a Supplier Fulfillment, retaining its identity and observed economic outcome. Recovering or replaying that attempt is not permission to create another purchase.
_Avoid_: customer Order, automatic retry order

**Ambiguous Supplier Purchase**:
A Supplier Purchase for which purchase or debit may have occurred but the outcome cannot yet be established. It blocks automatic replacement purchasing and refund until reliable evidence resolves the uncertainty.
_Avoid_: rejected purchase, presumed no debit

**Localized Product Copy**:
The per-locale variants of a Product's customer-facing catalog copy: Product Details and Replacement / Refund Policy, one variant per supported locale (`id`, `ms`, `en`), each edited separately in the Admin Dashboard and the Store Bot content editor. The base `description` column holds the Indonesian variant; `_ms`/`_en` columns hold the other variants. An empty variant renders the base copy, and it never changes another locale's text, price, stock, or premium-emoji metadata.
_Avoid_: machine translation, per-locale Product, translated catalog row

**Product Copy Variant**:
One locale's stored Product Details or Replacement / Refund Policy text together with that exact text's Telegram premium-emoji metadata. Metadata is bound to the variant it was captured from, so editing one locale's copy leaves every other locale's premium emoji intact.
_Avoid_: shared emoji offsets, copied translations
