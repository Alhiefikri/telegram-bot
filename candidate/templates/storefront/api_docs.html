Price quotes are informational. Order creation recalculates price and checks stock. Each order contains one Product, and catalog input_requirements is always empty. Delivery returns credentials from an authorized purchase, so treat its response as secret and do not log its contents.

Batas penggunaan

  • Body maksimum 32 KiB; jumlah per pesanan 1 sampai 100.
  • Halaman katalog dan pesanan 1 sampai 100, default 20. Halaman pengiriman 1 sampai 100, default 100.
  • Payload JSON pengiriman maksimum 1,047,552 byte; hasil yang tidak muat dengan aman menghasilkan temporarily_unavailable.
  • Idempotency-Key wajib untuk membuat pesanan, 1 sampai 128 karakter ASCII yang dapat dicetak.
  • Per akun: 120 pembacaan per 60 detik, 10 percobaan pesanan per 60 detik, dan 10 pembacaan pengiriman per 60 detik.
  • Per IP: 600 permintaan total dan 30 percobaan autentikasi hilang/tidak valid per 60 detik. Global: 300 pembacaan per detik dan 20 pesanan per detik; paling banyak 2 permit pesanan bersamaan per akun.

Request bodies are limited to 32 KiB and order quantity to 1–100. Catalog and order pages accept 1–100 rows (default 20); delivery pages accept 1–100 units (default 100) and a JSON payload of at most 1,047,552 bytes. Idempotency-Key is required for order creation and accepts 1–128 printable ASCII characters. Per account, limits are 120 reads, 10 order attempts, and 10 delivery reads per 60 seconds. Per IP, limits are 600 total requests and 30 missing/invalid authentication attempts per 60 seconds. Store-wide limits are 300 reads and 20 orders per second, with at most 2 concurrent order permits per account.
