## Required coverage and ambiguity boundaries

These are store acceptance requirements, **not verified CSxStore guarantees**. Before proposing history-derived no-debit, obtain provider-authored/account-scoped evidence that the records cover the exact account and original dispatch/acceptance interval; identify accepted/pending/debited/refunded orders; specify authoritative timezone and inclusive/exclusive bounds; establish ingestion-lag/visibility and retention guarantees; and prove all pages/filters/totals are complete under concurrent writes. Define the observation cutoff and when a negative result becomes final. Unknown identity, late acceptance, delayed history, page drift, missing pages, unknown clock/offset, filtered states or incomplete statements remain ambiguous.

For a case/statement decision, the provider response must identify the applicable reseller account non-secretly, original attempt/reference or exact order, covered time interval and timezone, ledger coverage, charged/refunded/net amounts and currency, and whether acceptance/debit remains possible after the cutoff. Record provider case ID, issue date and authority. Missing any material scope keeps no-debit unqualified; local operator confirmation must not fabricate that authority.
