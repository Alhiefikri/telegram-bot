# Operator-confirmed remainder refunds for externally funded full Order refunds

Status: Accepted and development-implemented, 2026-10-03. Extends [ADR-0017](0017-per-account-warranty-and-partial-refunds.md); resolves issue #525.

ADR-0017 requires every externally funded refund to carry exact amount/currency transfer evidence registered as an immutable `OrderRefund`, and `OrderStateMachine::applyPaymentRefunded()` refuses a full Order refund while any registered refund's confirmed total differs from the Payment amount. That guard had no mechanism for the unclaimed remainder: a Payment with one confirmed claim refund and a remaining amount that no verified claim or definitively missing fulfillment unit covers (supplier refusal, store goodwill) could never complete a full refund through Manual Fulfillment Review, leaving Review, Order and money out of sync.

The unclaimed remainder is now registered as one immutable `OrderRefund` in `confirmed` state at the moment an operator completes the full external refund, using the same exact transfer evidence (amount, currency, provider reference) they enter on the Review. The provider reference is stored encrypted with its unique hash identity, and an audit entry records the registration. Registration only records evidence: it never moves money, never credits Store Balance and never rewrites a confirmed partial refund. A Payment with registered refunds still `requested` or `uncertain` cannot complete the full refund until each is confirmed or recovered with its own reference; the guard message names that requirement. The remainder is the third origin shape on `order_refunds` (no warranty claim, account unit, fulfillment or missing quantity) and is unique per Payment, enforced by a partial unique index and the PostgreSQL origin check constraint. A full external refund with no registered refunds keeps its earlier behavior: the Review retains the operator evidence and no refund row is created. Store Balance refunds keep crediting only the remaining original debit through `StoreBalanceLedger::refund()`.

Rejected: offering only a cancel/reject action for pending `OrderRefund`s does not unblock the reported case because the remainder has no row to cancel; skipping or flag-bypassing the guard from the Review path would remove the database-visible invariant that a fully refunded external Payment has every refund confirmed and totaling the Payment; keeping remainder evidence only on the Review would leave the buyer-refund ledger incomplete. A separate operator screen for the remainder was rejected as an extra step for the same single evidence entry.

Consequences: external refund accounting is complete exactly when a Payment becomes `refunded`; operator monitoring shows the confirmed remainder beside missing-fulfillment refunds; the Review form remains evidence recording, never a transfer. This decision does not change refund finality, does not authorize refunding more than the original Payment, and does not make a failed or uncertain transfer disappear — those still require the existing confirm/recover path. Qualification: the system never executes a bank transfer; the operator remains the source of the exact reference.
