# Single-Owner IDR adjustments with transaction-bound confirmation

Status: **Accepted operational design; not runtime-implemented**, 2026-10-04. Requested under [H0 #538](https://github.com/Alhiefikri/telegram-bot/issues/538); approval evidence is in the [H0-v1 operational decision record](../balance-h0-operational-decisions.md). The Owner explicitly answered “setuju” after reviewing the linked ADR and accepting the residual risks of no independent approver, no business maximum and no mandatory payment proof, then approved the complete H0-v1 planning package. This exception to ADR-0019's distinct maker/approver rule covers manual IDR adjustments and linked full reversals only. Application actor mapping and downstream acceptance evidence remain required before activation; this is not permission to activate production funding.

The store has one Owner and no independent Admin/Finance approver. The Owner needs to add IDR to registered Telegram Customers for funds received and customer-selected refunds, including cases absent from system records. Requiring a second actor makes that operation unavailable. The proposed alternative binds each confirmation to fresh Owner password verification, an immutable preview, append-only history and durable alerts rather than silently granting a global Owner authorization bypass.

## Compensating controls

- Only the authenticated Owner can lookup, submit, confirm or reverse; server rechecks the current role/session and authenticated actor. The exception cannot grant Admin/Finance a self-approval path. Removing Owner access invalidates pending confirmations. Password verification is required for each posting/reversal, with no shared remembered password approval across transactions; never store password in request records, logs, queues or audit.
- Exact numeric Telegram ID identifies an existing Customer. Unknown IDs do not create Customers. Preview shows ID, stored username if available, IDR amount and reason. A server-side, single-use binding covers actor, Customer, amount, reason, source/reference and version. It expires five minutes after creation. Changed fields require a new preview; confirmed requests cannot be edited. Amount is positive integer IDR with checked ledger arithmetic, without a business maximum. Only the original approved amount may be posted.
- Five wrong password confirmations in fifteen minutes lock confirmation/reversal for that Owner for fifteen minutes. Count on request, check expiry by timestamp and send one durable lockout alert per lockout episode. A locked actor cannot post through alternate endpoints. Counter operations must be atomic; concurrent invalid attempts and runtime-state loss require qualification. A transaction rollback must not erase failure accounting; expensive password verification must be bounded. No scheduler per attempt/actor.
- Posting atomically appends the canonical IDR ledger entry, projection, request/source resolution, audit and required notification obligations. The audit keeps actor, recipient, currency/amount, reason, time, state and original/reversal reference during store operation without automatic deletion. No balance overwrite, history edit/delete or manual SQL repair.
- Recovery of a recorded Top-up/refund is linked to that original economic resolution. Manual recovery, provider callback, polling and automatic refund cannot each credit an independent source. All paths enforce the same DB uniqueness/row locks and immutable fingerprint. For an unrecorded case the Owner explicitly requires only a reason, without mandatory proof attachment/payment reference. It is an Owner-attested manual adjustment, not authenticated provider funding; do not fabricate an External Money Receipt or provider success. Request nonce/source identity prevents replay of that request, but cannot establish that independently submitted unlinked requests concern different physical funds. H0-v1 accepts that residual risk; honest counterpart accounting must be qualified downstream.
- Reversal cancels the entire original manual credit once, with fresh password, immutable reference and sufficient available IDR under account lock. Insufficient funds enters manual review; no negative balance or partial reversal. It does not erase the original ledger entry or claim an external payment was refunded. Linked Top-up/refund recovery must remain resolved to prevent later recredit.
- Customer notifications are mandatory for credits/reversals; Owner receives every manual credit/reversal and lockout alert. Reuse Telegram Outbound Effect and ambiguity review. A send failure cannot undo/repeat money. Account drift freezes affected mutations and alerts Owner; systemic drift pauses new funding. Recovery retains in-flight/ambiguous payment work.

## Additional acceptance before activation

| Requirement | Evidence required from downstream implementation |
| --- | --- |
| Single-Owner exception is deliberate | Final written Owner acceptance of this ADR, application actor/Telegram recipient mapping and completed H0 decision fields; no fake second approver |
| Authorization and confirmation | Wrong role/session/password, revoked role, expired preview, altered Customer/amount/reason/source and reused nonce cannot post; valid Owner confirmation works |
| Brute-force controls | Concurrent wrong attempts enforce the agreed threshold; lockout covers every posting/reversal entry point, expiry works and exactly one alert obligation per episode; password never serialized |
| Atomic and unique credit | Disposable PostgreSQL races: double-submit, callback/poll vs manual recovery, automatic vs manual refund, same receipt across targets and changed-payload replay; one source yields one ledger/accounting effect |
| Full reversal | Parallel reversal/reversal and reversal/spend produce one valid full reversal or insufficient-funds review; no negative balance, partial reversal or original-history mutation |
| Crash and Redis loss | Crash around commit/dispatch and Redis restart recover notification/work obligations from DB without lost money history or second credit |
| Audit and data safety | Owner-scoped paginated history, immutable rows, sanitized notification/audit data and documented retention/restore procedure |
| Lightweight operation | Indexed bounded history queries/recovery batches, request-triggered lockout, deduplicated alerts and measured query/worker overhead; no full-table polling or per-actor schedulers |

SQLite/sync tests alone do not establish race/crash guarantees. H0 specifies additional acceptance; downstream I1/I2/I3/V1 and operational gates prove it. Approval UI stays inactive until exception approval and applicable implementation acceptance are evidenced.

## Trade-off and scope

Owner reauthentication and audit increase resistance to accidental or session-only misuse, but do not replace independent approval if both Owner session and password are compromised. With no business maximum, one authorized action can create a large liability; the Owner explicitly accepted this residual risk in the linked-ADR interview sign-off. No independent reviewer is invented. Security incident handling can disable the exception; future staffing may restore maker-checker through a versioned policy decision.

This ADR does not authorize arbitrary promotional USDT grants, remove receipt attribution/finality, qualify Binance/custody, or redefine refund entitlements. The H0-v1 uncapped USDT exposure and all-user audience explicitly supersede the Proposed cap/beta policy in ADR-0019/#536; the linked implementation plan carries the amended contract downstream. H1/A1/H2 remain separate gates. Runtime policy remains DB/Admin; production remains GitHub Actions main/VPS.

Rejected: treating Owner role as universal bypass; reusing a password-confirmed session indefinitely; crediting an independent adjustment for an unresolved existing Top-up; deleting original entries on reversal; Redis-only money authority; scheduling one alert task for every password failure.
