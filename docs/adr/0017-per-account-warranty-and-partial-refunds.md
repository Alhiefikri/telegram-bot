# Per-account warranty and bounded partial buyer refunds

Status: Accepted and development-implemented, 2026-10-03. Production qualification remains separate.

The BuyMail reseller can deliver only part of a requested quantity and evaluates warranty claims per Gmail account. A buyer may choose a refund or replacement after verification. Reusing the full-order refund operation for one failed account would over-refund the buyer and retire healthy accounts.

We extend ADR-0008's **whole-order-only refund contract**, preserving its single StoreBalanceLedger mutation seam, append-only entries, original debit ownership and Customer account-first lock order. A partial buyer refund registers an immutable `OrderRefund` entitlement tied to the verified claim/unit, Payment, Customer and original retail snapshot. The ledger stores `source_type=order_refund`, its unique refund ID, and `refund_order_id`; account-locked cumulative credits cannot exceed the original matching purchase debit. A later whole-order refund credits only the remaining debit. Externally funded Orders retain their original refund rail and require exact amount/currency and confirmed transfer evidence; recording evidence never executes a bank transfer or credits Store Balance.

SupplierAccountUnit binds supplier connection/account identity, provider account ID, purchase, allocated credential, deadline, retail amount and logical unit number. Replacements preserve that logical entitlement and retire the original credential permanently. A durable SupplierPurchase with a warranty claim identity, stable reference and commitment owns replacement acquisition. Ambiguous acquisition blocks switching to refund and full-order refund until reliable evidence resolves it. A proven rejection or zero-debit/zero-delivery result permits switching to refund. Customer money is never spent for replacement.

Definitively missing units after bounded partial acquisition register a distinct, unique fulfillment-backed OrderRefund. Acquired-row quantity must match the settled supplier quantity, and all in-flight/ambiguous attempts must be resolved before this entitlement can exist. The refund returns only missing retail units and preserves delivered accounts. Confirmed refund quantity completes the remaining obligation; if all payment funds are returned, the ordinary full-refund lifecycle applies without publishing a successful sale. External remainder refunds require the same exact transfer evidence as account refunds.

Supplier claim state and customer settlement state are distinct. HTTP 202 means pending review, not approval. Authoritative GET establishes decisions; signed webhooks are durable read wakeups. A timed-out upload is recovered by GET; an operator may resubmit the stored original proof only when that authoritative GET holds no refund object for the same account, never automatically and never after observing a refund object. Approval reversal is stored as a new observation and operator liability, never an automatic debit against a buyer already refunded. The application never automatically confirms accounts or closes their warranty because a message was delivered.

Provider HTTP stays outside financial locks. Claim/settlement operations share Order/Payment/Product/Supplier/Catalog/Fulfillment/Purchase lock ownership with acquisition and full refunds, followed by account unit, stock item and claim locks. Store Balance settlement takes the Customer account before those resources. Unique database identities backstop application locks. Redis is a wakeup mechanism; PostgreSQL retains discoverable due work, dispatch intents and leases.

Private API keys and webhook secrets are encrypted and edited through Dashboard. Rotation verifies the same supplier profile identity outside the mutation transaction and rechecks its version before saving. Fixed-origin Bearer transport, disabled redirects and bounded decoding protect credentials. Proof uploads retain original bytes encrypted on private storage and are never flashed or published.

This decision extends ADR-0016 for per-unit warranty, partial acquisition and compensation. It does not promise a provider price lock, unrestricted refund coverage, guaranteed webhook delivery, refund finality, or exactly-once behavior without documented idempotency. API contracts not fully specified publicly require operator qualification.

## Terminal supplier observations (issue #530)

`reversed`, `rejected`, `cancelled` and locally `expired` stop periodic claim polling, but do not establish provider finality. Admin refresh and signed webhook wakeups may perform one leased, read-only authoritative account GET. They preserve the current state when GET contains no refund decision, record successful reads in the audit even when the decision is unchanged, and report failure when the read is skipped, fails validation/transport, or loses its lease. A later valid decision updates the observation/history without resetting customer settlement, crediting/debiting buyer funds, or unretiring credentials. Existing settlement identities continue to prevent a second refund.
