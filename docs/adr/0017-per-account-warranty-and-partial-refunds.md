# Historical per-account entitlements and bounded partial buyer refunds

Status: Accepted financial contract, 2026-10-03; provider-specific claim workflows retired 2026-10-19.

Historical per-account entitlements and definitively missing units require bounded partial refunds. Reusing a full-order refund for one failed account would over-refund the buyer and retire healthy accounts. New supplier-specific claim submission, replacement acquisition, account confirmation, deposits, webhook processing and warranty reminders are no longer supported.

We extend ADR-0008's **whole-order-only refund contract**, preserving its single StoreBalanceLedger mutation seam, append-only entries, original debit ownership and Customer account-first lock order. A partial buyer refund registers an immutable `OrderRefund` entitlement tied to the verified claim/unit, Payment, Customer and original retail snapshot. The ledger stores `source_type=order_refund`, its unique refund ID, and `refund_order_id`; account-locked cumulative credits cannot exceed the original matching purchase debit. A later whole-order refund credits only the remaining debit. Externally funded Orders retain their original refund rail and require exact amount/currency and confirmed transfer evidence; recording evidence never executes a bank transfer or credits Store Balance.

`SupplierAccountUnit` retains supplier connection/account identity, provider account ID, purchase, allocated credential, deadline, retail amount and logical unit number for historical Orders. Existing replacement purchases and settlement decisions remain history, not an authorization to acquire another account. Ambiguous acquisition blocks buyer compensation and full-order refunds until reliable evidence resolves it. Customer money is never spent again to resolve historical replacement uncertainty, and retired credentials never return to inventory.

Definitively missing units after bounded partial acquisition register a distinct, unique fulfillment-backed OrderRefund. Acquired-row quantity must match the settled supplier quantity, and all in-flight/ambiguous attempts must be resolved before this entitlement can exist. The refund returns only missing retail units and preserves delivered accounts. Confirmed refund quantity completes the remaining obligation; if all payment funds are returned, the ordinary full-refund lifecycle applies without publishing a successful sale. External remainder refunds require the same exact transfer evidence as account refunds.

Historical supplier decision and customer settlement states remain distinct. A recorded supplier reversal never automatically debits a buyer already refunded or un-retires a credential. Existing verified entitlements retain their one-refund identity and original payment currency; retirement does not erase financial obligations.

Provider HTTP stays outside financial locks. Historical settlement shares Order/Payment/Product/Supplier/Catalog/Fulfillment/Purchase lock ownership with acquisition and full refunds, followed by account unit, stock item and claim locks. Store Balance settlement takes the Customer account before those resources. Unique database identities backstop application locks.

Private API keys and retained historical credential data remain encrypted. Disabling unsupported supplier connections and mappings must preserve purchases, holds, refund records, account units and local stock. A rollback must not reactivate unsupported providers automatically.

This decision extends ADR-0016 for historical per-unit entitlements and compensation. It does not promise a provider price lock, unrestricted refund coverage, guaranteed delivery, refund finality, or exactly-once behavior without documented idempotency. Supported supplier API contracts still require operator qualification.

Implementation extension (U11 #556, 2026-10-06): internal `usdt_balance` Payments retain the same account-before-Order/resource lock graph, with the separate `CustomerUsdtBalanceLedger` as their sole money writer. `UsdtOrderRefundService` validates the original USDT Customer/Payment/debit fingerprint and frozen per-unit USDT allocation, and full refunds credit only its remaining micro-USDT debit. Registered internal entitlements cannot also confirm external transfer evidence. Immutable credits/accounting and exact durable Telegram refund receipts commit with lifecycle settlement; ambiguous supplier or delivery evidence stays reviewable. This technical implementation does not authorize production funding or replace IDR/external refund rails.
