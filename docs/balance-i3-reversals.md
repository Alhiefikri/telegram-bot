# IDR adjustment reversal boundary (#545)

I3 implements the linked full reversal of one applied IDR adjustment for [issue #545](https://github.com/Alhiefikri/telegram-bot/issues/545), using the accepted [H0-v1 single-Owner overlay / ADR-0020](https://github.com/Alhiefikri/telegram-bot/blob/9dbbaa80/docs/adr/0020-single-owner-balance-adjustments.md) and the [ten-stage H0-v1 plan](https://github.com/Alhiefikri/telegram-bot/blob/9dbbaa80/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-04-balance-h0-implementation-plan.md). Dependency I1 #543 is closed and its request/password boundary is reused unchanged. No deployment, production activation or USDT funding is authorized by I3.

`BalanceAdjustmentService::requestReversal()` requires the authenticated current Owner, a started session and the exact numeric Telegram ID of the Customer shown in the history. It server-loads the original `BalanceAdjustmentRequest` and verifies that it is `applied`, IDR, has a linked credit entry of the kind its category posted (`admin_adjustment_credit` for `owner_attested`/`funded_transfer`/`promotional_grant`/`correction`, `owner_compensation_credit` for `top_up_recovery`, `refund_credit` for `refund_recovery`) and that the entry amount, account and Customer match. The new preview is a `category = 'reversal'` request whose `amount` is the full original credit and whose `source_id` is the original request; the Owner never supplies the reversed amount, so there is no partial reversal. The preview is immutable, expires after five minutes, snapshots the current Customer Telegram identity, and rejects a foreign Customer, an unknown or non-adjustment source, a non-IDR original, a second open preview and an already-reversed entitlement. The open-preview check is sequential; two concurrent previews for one entitlement are still resolved safely at confirmation by the unique ledger claim (one debit, the other `original_already_reversed`, no partial accounting).

`confirm()` for a reversal reuses the exact I1 per-action password boundary: the same KDF lease, durable five-failure/15-minute lockout, session/credential/version/fingerprint binding and replay semantics, with no second password endpoint. Inside one money transaction it locks Customer → account → reversal preview → original request, re-verifies the original linkage, and calls `StoreBalanceLedger::reverseApprovedAdjustment()`, which posts one `admin_adjustment_reversal` debit under the account lock with the **full-history** projection check. The unique `(source_type, source_id, kind)` ledger claim on `balance_adjustment` + original request id guarantees at most one reversal entry per entitlement; a matching replay returns the original entry, and a concurrent losing preview is rejected with `original_already_reversed` before any accounting row is written. The request/confirm flows share one `BalanceAdjustmentService::isReversibleOriginal()` predicate and one `openReversalSourceIds()` open-preview query, so the Dashboard button and the service cannot drift.

Available funds are checked against the locked projection: an insufficient balance rejects as `reversal_insufficient_funds`, rolls the whole transaction back, appends one durable `balance.adjustment.reversal_review` audit row, and leaves the preview pending for review/renewal — there is no partial debit, no negative balance and no hidden write. The debit posts one balanced accounting counterpart (`customer_idr_liability` against the original counterpart account; a `funded_transfer` reversal credits `adjustment_reversal_gain` instead of de-recognizing `verified_settlement_asset`, because the external money was not returned), the applied audit row, one Customer reversal notice and one Owner alert inside the same transaction; notifications materialize through `TelegramOutboundEffectService` and recover through `telegram:recover-outbound`.

Reversing a linked `top_up_recovery` or `refund_recovery` credit does not reopen its source: the original `StoreBalanceCompensation`/`OrderRefund` resolution, the top-up status and any claimed `VerifiedMoneyReceipt` remain resolved, so late provider evidence or callbacks still resolve without a second credit, and the same physical funds cannot fund a new adjustment. Reversal entries, the reversal request and its accounting/audit rows are append-only under the existing PostgreSQL adjustment-history trigger.

All signed ledger consumers understand the reversal debit direction through the central `StoreBalanceEntryKind::debitValues()` mapping: the ledger projection check, `StoreBalanceReconciliation` (`store-balance:check`), the Telegram Customer activity feed (`adjustment_reversal` kind, `debited` state, localized id/en/ms) and the Admin history direction rendering. The Dashboard adds the Owner-only `admin.store-balance.adjustments.reversals.store` route and a full-reversal action on eligible applied history rows; confirmation and cancellation reuse the existing preview routes.

## Verification evidence (2026-10-05)

Implemented against `main` with only disposable/local resources; no production I/O, deployment or funding. Focused `AdminBalanceAdjustment*` lane: 42 tests, 340 assertions, no failures (12 PostgreSQL-only races skipped in the SQLite lane). Full `php artisan test --parallel`: 3,425 tests, 25,584 assertions, no failures; 120 PostgreSQL-only tests skipped. Disposable PostgreSQL 16 `scripts/concurrency-deploy-gate-local.sh`: 167 tests, 1,489 assertions, no failures/errors/skips; gate report 147 tests, 1,330 assertions. Focused `AdminBalanceAdjustmentPostgresConcurrencyTest` on the disposable PostgreSQL database: 12 tests, 132 assertions.

The PostgreSQL races prove: reversal vs purchase debit (exactly one side wins the account lock, balance never negative, projection matches the signed ledger), double reversal via two live previews (one linked debit and one accounting counterpart; the loser resolves as `original_already_reversed` on retry), reversal vs refund credit (one refund, at most one reversal, final projection consistent), and reversal vs late Top-up settlement (compensated money never recredited, receipt reservation resolved). PostgreSQL triggers reject edits/deletes of the reversal request identity, reversal ledger entry, accounting event and adjustment audit rows. Feature coverage includes full-amount-only linkage, foreign/non-adjustment/non-IDR/non-applied source denial, replay returning the same entry, insufficient-funds safe review with durable `balance.adjustment.reversal_review` audit and no partial write, rollback of accounting failure, projection drift failing closed, recovery-source preservation and receipt non-release, notification recovery, and Dashboard create/confirm/history rendering. Focused browser check `admin balance adjustment previews the exact Customer and disables duplicate confirmation` passes; Pint passes.

Limitations: SQLite/sync tests do not prove locking or trigger behavior — only the disposable PostgreSQL gate above does. Two concurrent reversal previews are possible because the open-preview check is sequential; the unique ledger claim makes that race economically safe. Production Owner-to-Telegram recipient mapping, provider/custody qualification and V1/V2 operational qualification remain separate activation gates.

## Boundaries

I3 does not refund external money, release receipt claims, delete or edit any original entry, support partial reversal, allow reversal of a reversal, or reopen compensated/refunded sources. Production Owner-to-Telegram recipient mapping, provider/custody qualification and V1/V2 operational qualification remain separate activation gates.
